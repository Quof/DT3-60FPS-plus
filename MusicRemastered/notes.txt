CentralCityA

Moogy: Not entirely pleased with how this one came out. Zephyr used a game rip instead of the OST version, and the OST version sounds meaningfully different. As such, I tracked down a PSF source (118 on this page) myself, but Audio Overload proved unable to handle the file, producing garbled audio upon playback. I opted to convert the mp3 available on Zophar's Domain into ogg and cut the length for parity with Zephyr's, boosting the volume slightly in the process. The resultant file sounds better than the original, but still has some noticeable degradation. My recommendation would be to swap my file in for now, but consider finding a way to directly convert the PSF OR simply use the OST version of the song instead, which is available in FLAC.
