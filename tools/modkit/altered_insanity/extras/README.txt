ALTERED INSANITY for DT3+
=========================

Altered Insanity is cousinoer5's unofficial DLC for Distorted Travesty 3 (version 1.01). This folder is its Virtual
Insanity part converted into a DT3+ mod. Its Altered Beast part (the alternate equipment set) is a mod of its own,
AlteredBeast: the two work on their own or together.

To play: put this folder in DT3+'s Mods folder, turn it on on the title screen (Mods), and start the game again.
Everything new begins in the Bubble Tower B-side. Keep an eye on Mystery.txt in the game's folder: it holds the hint
for your next objective.

The original recommends playing it with everything collected, on normal mode. saves\ has the two saves it came with:
everything collected before the DLC, and with Virtual Insanity completed. To use one, copy it into DT3+'s Saves folder
as an empty slot's file (DT3data2.dts to DT3data5.dts; slot 1 is DT3data.dts). Saves from the original load in DT3+
(the mod reads its progress from them); DT3+ keeps the mod's progress in its own section of the save file.


WHAT'S HERE
-----------

Virtual Insanity: its rooms (the challenge hub, the arenas and their Challenge Master, the puzzles, the piano, the
no-walking areas, the boss halls and boss challenges), objects, scripts, sprites, font, sound effects and music, the
ways into it from the game's own rooms (the Bubble Tower B-side, Central City, the Great Palace, the Sacred Temple,
the Barrens' desert puzzle and a door in the main world), its title screen codes, its game over hints and retries, its
boss challenge timer and hit penalty, and Elpizo's new music in the extra gate.

The game's own objects it changed are copies (clones, named <object>__AI) used only in its own rooms, so the game's
objects stay as DT3+ has them. It runs as a 30fps mod: its code runs in 30fps steps, as it was made to.


WHAT ISN'T
----------

These were changes to the game's own scripts and system objects (the HUD, the pause menu, the Chao), which this mod
doesn't change:

  Speedrun Hall       the per-chapter speedrun mode (its entrance in the boss gallery and its signs are left out)
  Chao challenges     playing as the Chao outside the Abomination fight (the doors to Chao's Warmaster, CHEX, CHEXMIX,
                      Chao and the Chao training room are left out)

and some smaller changes: the names taken off a few enemies (rMountTemple_B, C and G), and the original's changes to
other game objects outside its own rooms (unported\objects lists them; the Altered Beast mod has its own).

report.txt says what was converted and how; unported\ has the original's versions of what wasn't, with what it changed.


Altered Insanity is by cousinoer5. Where a track's source is known, the pause menu credits it while it plays.
