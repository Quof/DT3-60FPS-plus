ALTERED BEAST for DT3+
======================

Altered Beast is the alternate equipment set from cousinoer5's Altered Insanity, an unofficial DLC for Distorted
Travesty 3 (version 1.01), made into a DT3+ mod.

To play: put this folder in DT3+'s Mods folder, turn it on on the title screen (Mods), and start the game again. Then
either type alterme on the title screen (press Enter for the code prompt) before starting a new game, to start with
every piece of equipment, altered; or, once the game is beaten, find the Altered Beast on Begonia's beach and talk to
it to switch your save between the altered equipment and the normal one. (The title screen's list of codes doesn't
show alterme.)

Every piece of equipment has a new effect when altered. The Mystery Tiara gives each weapon a special attack
(Character Swap with an attack button), paid for in green health: life you lose when you're hit, and win back by dealing
damage. There are curses, true damage and options for some pieces in the pause menu. Once you've met the Altered Beast,
a door on a ledge high up in the middle of the Bubble Tower B-side's hub leads to its room: a sign about each piece of equipment and
each special attack, and some altered enemies to look at.


HOW IT'S MADE
-------------

Altered Beast changed the game's own scripts and objects: the player, the weapons, the bosses, the HUD, the pause menu.
This mod has DT3+'s versions of those with Altered Insanity's changes merged in, made to work at 60 and 120fps, and they
replace the game's while the mod is on. Its new objects (the special attacks, the altered enemies) run as 30fps code, as
they were made.

So it's made for this version of DT3+: a later DT3+ that changes the same scripts or objects needs the mod made again
from it (tools\modkit in the DT3+ project does that).

Turned off, the game is DT3+ as it was, and saves keep the mod's data for when it's on again. Equipment found stays
found, alterme's too.

It works with the Altered Insanity mod (Virtual Insanity) on as well.

report.txt says what's in the mod and how it was made.


Altered Insanity is by cousinoer5.
