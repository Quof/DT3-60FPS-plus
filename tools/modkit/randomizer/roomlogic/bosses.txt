# What each boss fight takes. solve.py reads this with overrides.txt: going into a boss's room from another room is its
# fight, and needs this (a boss room not here needs any attack); a "via" line is an exit whose going through starts the
# fight (the story's exit goes on past the boss's room in an endgame world). Lines:
#   <room> : <requirements> [@ N%]                       the fight in that room
#   via <room> out <x,y> : <requirements> [@ N%]         the fight going through that exit starts
# Requirements as in overrides.txt ("attack": link 1 | belmont 1 | megaman 1 | samus 1). "@ N%": the fight also waits
# till N% of all the checks are in logic (pct N: the room logic's reach counts them, every game's), so a seed never has
# a boss to beat on the way to the items before that much of the game is open; the player needn't have taken them. With
# Boss Rando it goes with the boss, wherever it's fought.
#
# Worked out from the bosses' damage resistances (takeDamage: each boss's resType for the six damage types; 1 is 0x,
# 2 0.5x, 4 2x, 5 3x, anything else 1x). The player's weapons and their types:
#   NORMAL     link 1 (Master Sword), belmont 1 (Hair Whip)
#   PIERCE     link 2 (arrows), belmont 2 (dagger)
#   ELEMENTAL  belmont 3 (Holy Water), megaman 2 (Shotgun Ice)
#   SHOT       megaman 1 (X Buster), samus 1 (Arm Cannon; the Charge Beam and Spazer too)
#   EXPLOSION  link 3 (Fuse Bomb), samus 6 (Bombs)
#   SPECIAL    megaman 3 (Gravity Well), samus 4 (Missiles)
#   and in Gate 6 and The Executive's fight, the only places its sets work: gamec 1 (Blade Strike) NORMAL, gamec 2
#   (Chainblade Storm) PIERCE, gamej 1 (Spam Gun) SHOT, gamej 2 (Bird Swarm) SPECIAL and EXPLOSION. The Strike Chain
#   (megaman 4) does no damage.
# A type counts if the boss takes it at 1x or more as the fight starts and no phase of the fight takes it down to 0x (a
# moment when nothing at all hurts the boss isn't a phase). A fight with parts (two bosses, a shield to break) needs a
# weapon for each. Fights that need more than that are written by hand, with why.
# The shares, and the needs that aren't damage (the Mario Ability Set for Giant Blargg, the Strike Chain for the
# Weapons Facility's two), are set by hand; each boss's number in the comments is its place in the Item Tracker's boss
# list.
