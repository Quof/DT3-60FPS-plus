# DT3+

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export
a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

DT3+ is played through its Randomizer mod. A new game starts in the open endgame world (every area open, the story
done, no CHAOS invasion) in the first room, with nothing but walking. Every collectible the pause menu's progress page
counts (heart pieces and containers, treasure chests, arrow quivers, heart energy, missile tanks, Game Power, air
upgrades, Internetz mods, weapon upgrades, skill points, bomb upgrades, keys, the Item Finder, the X-Special charge and
the Spring Ball), every movement ability (jump, dash, air dash, double jump, the High Jump Boots) and every ability set
(the Mario Ability Set, Link, Belmont, Mega Man, Samus and the two Gate 6 sets, progressively) is shuffled into the
multiworld. Three more locations lie on the ground where the game starts. Each key opens one locked door of its own.

Bosses are fought in their own rooms with the abilities found so far, and must be beaten to go past them. Tower defense
and the Trial Master's cursed trials have to be done again for their rewards. The equipment shops' stock is shuffled
between the shops.

## What is the goal of DT3+ when randomized?

Get the 8 instruments (the Nightmare Gates' bosses, then the Sacred Temple), beat the Abomination, then defeat the CHAOS
Warmaster, whose door appears in the first room once the Abomination is beaten.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

The 544 locations of the Randomizer: every pickup and treasure chest in the game's rooms, the heart containers the
bosses and the 7th trial give, and three starting locations. The items are what those hold, plus every ability the
story would have given.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Any of them.

## What does another world's item look like in DT3+?

A multicoloured Archipelago orb. Touching it sends the item to its player; your own items show as themselves.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

It's given at once, with a message saying what it is and who found it.

## Is the logic exact?

The logic is an estimate from the story's order: a location is expected to need what the story had given by the time
it first reaches that location's area. Places needing an ability from later in the story than their area may be out of
logic.
